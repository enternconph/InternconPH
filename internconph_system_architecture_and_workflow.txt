   1. SYSTEM GOALS & STRATEGIC OBJECTIVES (WHAT THE SYSTEM IS TRYING TO ACHIEVE)
   ====================================================================================================

   The overarching mission of InternConPH is to solve the fragmented, high-risk, and manually cumbersome
   internship (On-the-Job Training - OJT) and entry-level employment ecosystem in the Philippines.

   InternConPH is designed to achieve the following specific goals:

   1. ELIMINATE FRAUDULENT EMPLOYERS & DIPLOMA MILLS:
      Unaccredited schools and illegitimate fly-by-night businesses exploit student interns as free labor
      or expose them to scam recruitments. InternConPH implements a strict legal legitimacy gate requiring
      SEC/DTI registration, Mayor's Business Permits, and BIR Form 2303 for companies, as well as CHED
      Government Recognition (GR), PACUCOA/AACCUP/PAASCU accreditation, and TESDA CTPR for institutions.

   2. ENFORCE LEGAL SIPP COMPLIANCE (CHED CMO NO. 104, SERIES OF 2017):
      The Student Internship Program in the Philippines (SIPP) requires a tripartite consensus between
      the Higher Education Institution (HEI), the Host Training Establishment (HTE/Organization), and
      the Student Intern. InternConPH ensures no student can be deployed to an OJT unless their academic
      institution has formally approved both the corporate partner and the specific internship curriculum.

   3. PREVENT STUDENT EXPLOITATION & TRACK TRAINING HOURS:
      Interns often suffer from ghost hours, unverified overwork, or unrelated manual labor. The system
      implements a tamper-proof Daily Time Record (DTR) and attendance logging protocol where assigned
      Workplace Mentors certify exact clock-in, clock-out, rendered hours, and tasks accomplished against
      the student's required curriculum quota (e.g. 300, 486, or 600 hours).

   4. BRIDGING THE GAP: FROM OJT INTERN TO EMPLOYABLE PROFESSIONAL:
      Instead of internships ending in a paper certificate lost in a folder, InternConPH transitions
      students across dynamic lifecycle states:
      [Starting OJT] -> [Ongoing Intern] -> [OJT Completer] -> [Graduated Alumni / Career Candidate]
      Every completed OJT record, positive employer performance evaluation, and on-call engagement is
      automatically converted into cryptographically verified items on the student's lifelong Career Portfolio.

   5. PROVIDE DATA PRIVACY & ANONYMIZED STUDENT GRIEVANCE SHIELDING:
      In compliance with Republic Act 10173 (Philippine Data Privacy Act), students who experience workplace
      harassment, labor violations, or safety issues can file formal complaints that route exclusively to
      their university's Guidance Counselor and OJT Supervisor. The system can mask the student's personal
      identity when notifying the employer to protect the student from retaliation during active OJT.

   2. SYSTEM USERS & ROLES HIERARCHY
   ====================================================================================================

   The platform governs 6 primary user groups across distinct operational scopes:

   [1] SYSTEM ADMINISTRATOR (Role: 'system_admin')
      - The highest authority of the platform.
      - Inspects and verifies uploaded government proof-of-legitimacy documents.
      - Approves or rejects Institution Director and Hiring Organization registrations.
      - Audits nation-wide analytics, skill supply/demand dynamics, and platform-wide audit logs.
      - Governs systemic disputes escalated by academic institutions against errant employers.

   [2] INSTITUTION DIRECTOR (Role: 'institution')
      - The principal executive / OJT Director / Dean representing the College or University.
      - Manages the institution profile, campus address, and degree programs/curricula.
      - Generates cryptographically hashed access passcodes to invite Institution Staff.
      - Reviews, approves, or rejects OJT and Job opportunities offered by partner organizations.

   [3] INSTITUTION STAFF (Role: 'institution_staff')
      Scoped by precise academic positions with granular permissions:
      a) OJT Supervisor / Coordinator:
         - Monitors assigned students during active deployment.
         - Reviews weekly and daily attendance progress, hours remaining, and performance evaluations.
         - Reviews organization-filed incident complaints and issues academic student warnings.
      b) College Registrar:
         - Verifies student identities, student numbers, and curriculum enrollment.
         - Evaluates Returnees and Transferees requiring special scholastic clearance.
         - Manages clearance requirement checklists and academic certifications.
      c) Guidance Counselor:
         - Handles formal student grievances, complaints against organizations, and safety reports.
         - Conducts mediation and shields student whistleblower identities when necessary.
      d) Academic Dean:
         - High-level oversight across faculty staff, student rosters, and departmental programs.
         - Approves department-wide internship memorandums and partner tie-ups.

   [4] HIRING ORGANIZATION / HR ADMINISTRATOR (Role: 'hiring_organization' / 'hr_staff')
      - The official corporate or business account representing the Host Training Establishment (HTE).
      - Posts opportunities across three distinct tracks:
         * OJT Opportunities (strictly for eligible students)
         * Career Jobs (strictly for graduated alumni)
         * On-Call Projects (gig/deliverable-based fast credits)
      - Targets specific universities and academic programs for each opportunity.
      - Evaluates applicant pools, schedules interviews, issues formal job/OJT offers.
      - Generates Workplace Mentor invitations and assigns them to active interns.

   [5] WORKPLACE MENTOR / SUPERVISOR (Role: 'workplace_mentor' / 'mentor')
      - Department heads, team leads, or engineers within the hiring organization.
      - Oversees the day-to-day work tasks of the deployed student intern.
      - Certifies Daily Time Records (DTR), verifies clock-ins/outs, and credits training hours.
      - Submits Midterm and Final OJT Performance Evaluations with rubric scores.
      - Files formal Incident / Misconduct / Accident reports directly to the university coordinator.

   [6] STUDENT CANDIDATE (Role: 'student')
      Classified by enrollment type: Regular, Returnee, Transferee.
      Progresses dynamically through lifecycle states:
      a) OJT Student (Starting / Ongoing):
         - Qualified exclusively to browse and apply for approved OJT internships matching their degree.
         - Locked out of applying to full-time career positions until graduation.
         - Logs daily tasks, tracks approved hours, and views assigned supervisors.
      b) OJT Completer:
         - Has fulfilled all mandatory OJT hours (e.g., 486/486 hrs) verified by their mentor.
         - Unlocks completion credentials and verified employer recommendation badges.
      c) Graduated Student / Alumni:
         - Fully unlocked access to browse and apply for full-time and part-time Career Jobs.
         - Manages a dynamic, verified career portfolio with authenticated workplace evidence.

   3. COMPREHENSIVE STEP-BY-STEP USER FLOWS
   ====================================================================================================

   ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   FLOW 1: REGISTRATION, PROOF OF LEGITIMACY & ADMIN VERIFICATION
   ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   1. An Institution Director or Hiring Organization HR navigates to the registration portal.
   2. The registration workflow enforces geographic and legal precision:
      - For Companies: Legal entity type (Corporation, Sole Proprietorship, Partnership).
      Must upload: SEC / DTI Certificate, Mayor's Business Permit, BIR Form 2303 (Tax Reg).
      - For HEIs / Universities: Institutional category (State University, Private HEI, Tech-Voc).
      Must upload: CHED Government Recognition (GR), Autonomous Deregulated Charter, or DepEd/TESDA CTPR.
   3. Upon submission:
      - An account record is created in `users` with `is_active = 0` (or `status = 'pending'`).
      - The entity is logged into `entity_registrations` with uploaded documents in `institution_documents`
      or `organization_registrations`.
   4. The System Admin logs into `/dashboard/admin`:
      - Admin inspects the submitted company/school dossier, downloads and examines verification PDFs.
      - Admin clicks "Verify & Approve" or "Reject with Cause".
      - If Approved: `is_verified` is set to 1, `status = 'active'`, and a realtime WebSocket notification
      is pushed to the entity.
      - If Rejected: Detailed rejection reasons are saved to the audit log, the applicant is notified,
      and restricted features remain strictly locked.

   ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   FLOW 2: ACCESS CODE & DELEGATED PASSCODE GOVERNANCE
   ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   To eliminate unauthorized registrations, all sub-accounts (Staff, Mentors, Students) are governed by
   cryptographic Access Codes created by their respective governing authorities:

   1. Director -> Staff Access Code:
      - Institution Director defines: Staff ID/Number, Intended Position (Supervisor, Registrar, etc.),
      Department/Program assignment, and customized JSON permission flags.
      - The backend hashes and stores the passcode in `access_codes` with an expiration timestamp.
   2. HR -> Mentor Access Code:
      - HR creates a passcode specifying the Mentor's Employee ID, Corporate Email, and Department.
   3. Staff -> Student Access Code:
      - The OJT Coordinator generates access codes linked to the Student's School ID number and Program.
   4. Validation during Registration:
      - When a user inputs an access code during signup, the backend checks:
      * Code exists, is unexpired (`expires_at > NOW()`), and has not been used (`is_used = 0`).
      * Target identifier matches the applicant's entered Student ID / Employee ID.
      * Program and institution match the issuer.
      - Upon successful signup, `access_codes.is_used` is set to 1, and the code is linked to `used_by_user_id`.

   ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   FLOW 3: INSTITUTION STAFF SCOPED OPERATIONS & STUDENT INTAKE
   ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   1. When a Student registers using an institution access code:
      - The student selects their academic degree, classification (Regular, Returnee, Transferee),
      and initial OJT status (Starting OJT or Completed OJT).
   2. The registration lands on the Institution Staff dashboard:
      - Regular students are routed to the OJT Supervisor matching their program code.
      - Returnees and Transferees are routed to the Registrar for transcript and prerequisite validation.
   3. The Staff member cross-checks the student's credentials against the institutional registrar database:
      - Staff confirms hours required (e.g., BSIT = 486 hrs, BSBA = 600 hrs, BSHM = 500 hrs).
      - Once approved, the student's status is officially marked `verified` and their dashboard unlocks.

   ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   FLOW 4: OPPORTUNITY CREATION & INSTITUTIONAL CLEARANCE (OJT VS. CAREER VS. ON-CALL)
   ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   InternConPH enforces strict separation between OJT Training and Professional Labor:

   1. HR drafts an Opportunity in `/dashboard/organization/jobs`:
      - Selects Posting Type:
      * 'ojt' (Internship training; requires student status; target audience: OJT Students)
      * 'career_job' (Full-time / Part-time employment; target audience: Graduated Students)
      * 'on_call' (Short-term deliverable projects; stipend-based; credits portfolio)
      - Configures work setup (Onsite, Remote, Hybrid), available slots, salary/allowance rate,
      required skills, and deadline/finish time.
      - Designates the Workplace Mentor who will oversee successful candidates.
   2. Institutional Tripartite Clearance:
      - HR selects which Higher Education Institutions are invited to receive this posting.
      - The posting inserts pending records into `institution_job_approvals`.
      - The posting CANNOT be seen by any student until the Institution Director or Dean approves it!
   3. Once the institution approves the opportunity:
      - The system queries `job_required_programs` and matches student curricula.
      - Real-time notifications alert eligible students that an approved partnership opening is live.

   ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   FLOW 5: CANDIDATE MATCHING, APPLICATION LIFECYCLE & INTERVIEW PIPELINES
   ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   1. Student Discovery:
      - An OJT student browses opportunities. If they attempt to apply for a 'career_job', the system
      blocks the submission with an alert: "Career jobs are exclusively reserved for Graduated Alumni."
      - The system calculates an algorithm match score based on shared skills in `student_skills` vs.
      `job_required_skills`.
   2. Application Submission:
      - Student submits their resume (`student_resumes`), cover letter, and verified portfolio items.
      - Application record enters `job_applications` with status 'pending'.
   3. HR Review & Interview Stage:
      - HR reviews candidate profiles, academic history, GPA, and verified past badges.
      - HR updates status: 'reviewing' -> 'shortlisted'.
      - HR triggers "Request Interview":
      * Sets date/time, mode ('onsite', 'online', 'phone'), location/meeting link, and notes.
      * Record created in `interviews` table. Real-time notification delivered to student.
      - Student accepts or attends interview.
   4. Formal Offer & Acceptance:
      - If selected for OJT: HR issues an offer recorded in `ojt_deployment_offers`.
      - The Institution Coordinator approves the deployment contract.
      - Student accepts the offer: `ojt_records` is created, initializing their official training program.

   ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   FLOW 6: ACTIVE OJT DEPLOYMENT, DTR DAILY ATTENDANCE & HOUR VERIFICATION
   ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   1. Initialization of Training:
      - `ojt_records` stores: `student_id`, `organization_id`, `program_id`, `start_date`,
      `required_hours` (e.g. 486.00), and `rendered_hours` (initially 0.00).
   2. Daily Time Record (DTR) Clocking:
      - Each training day, the intern records their shift details and tasks accomplished.
      - A log entry is created in `ojt_attendance_logs`: `log_date`, `time_in`, `time_out`,
      `tasks_accomplished`, and `hours_rendered`. Status defaults to 'pending'.
   3. Supervisor / Workplace Mentor Certification:
      - The assigned Workplace Mentor logs into `/dashboard/organization/ojt`.
      - The mentor reviews the hours and tasks.
      - Mentor clicks "Verify & Credit":
      * `ojt_attendance_logs.status` becomes 'verified'.
      * `ojt_records.rendered_hours` is automatically incremented.
      * `students.completed_ojt_hours` is synchronized.
      * Student receives an instant notification of credited hours and remaining balance.
      - Or Mentor clicks "Add Manual Hours" via `POST /api/org/interns/:ojtId/hours` to credit
      special projects or overtime with automated audit logs.

   ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   FLOW 7: PERFORMANCE EVALUATION & VERIFIED CAREER PORTFOLIO GENERATION
   ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   1. Completion Milestone:
      - When `rendered_hours >= required_hours`, the student reaches 'completed' status.
   2. Employer Evaluation:
      - Workplace Mentor submits Midterm / Final evaluation in `ojt_performance_records`.
      - Evaluates standardized rubric criteria:
      * Quality of Work, Dependability & Initiative, Technical Skill Mastery, Workplace Ethics.
      - Calculates numeric rating (e.g. 4.85 / 5.00) with written qualitative supervisor commendations.
   3. Automated Portfolio Crediting:
      - Upon OJT completion or On-Call deliverable acceptance (`/applicants/:id/accept-on-call`),
      the system automatically generates an authenticated record in `portfolio_items`:
      * `title`: "Certified OJT: [Position Title]" or "On-Call Experience: [Project Name]"
      * `associated_org_id`: verified hiring organization ID
      * `is_verified`: 1 (Officially authenticated by corporate partner)
      - When the student graduates and applies for career jobs, prospective HR recruiters can inspect
      these verified credentials with zero risk of fabricated resumes.

   ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   FLOW 8: INCIDENT REPORTING, COMPLAINTS, ACCIDENT RECORDS & GRIEVANCE SHIELDING
   ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   InternConPH provides bidirectional accountability with structured conflict resolution:

   A. Employer Filing Against Intern:
      - If an intern violates safety protocols, commits unexcused absences, or breaches conduct,
      the Workplace Mentor/HR files a report via `POST /api/org/complaints`.
      - If physical injury occurred, an `accident_reports` record is linked (capturing injury description,
      medical attention given, witnesses, and preventive measures).
      - This complaint is transmitted EXCLUSIVELY to the student's academic institution coordinator.
      - The coordinator investigates, schedules a conference, and issues an institutional warning note.

   B. Student Filing Grievance Against Employer (With Whistleblower Protection):
      - If a student encounters sexual harassment, unpaid overtime, safety hazards, or recruiter ghosting,
      the student files a grievance via `POST /api/student/complaints`.
      - The complaint is routed directly to the University Guidance Counselor.
      - Privacy Shielding: When the university forwards the notice to the organization for formal response,
      the university can toggle `include_student_details = 0`. The company sees "Student Intern
      (Identity Protected by Institution)", preventing retaliation while active on site.
      - If an organization exhibits repeat critical violations, the Institution escalates the dossier
      to the System Administrator to revoke the company's nationwide platform accreditation.

   [users] 1 --- * [roles]
      |

      |               |                              |

      |

   |                              ACCESS CODES & INVITATION DELEGATION                                |

      - code_hash (Unique cryptographic identifier)

   |                             OPPORTUNITY & TRIPARTITE CLEARANCE                                   |

         +--- 1:* --- [job_applications]
                           |
                           +--- 1:* --- [interviews]
                           |
                           +--- 1:1 --- [job_offers] (For Career Jobs)
                           |
                           +--- 1:1 --- [ojt_deployment_offers] (For OJT Internships)

   +--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
   |                             ACTIVE OJT DEPLOYMENT & TIME TRACKING                                |
   +--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
   [students] 1 --- * [ojt_records] * --- 1 [hiring_organizations]
                              |
                              +--- 1:* --- [ojt_attendance_logs] (Daily DTR records)
                              |               - log_date, time_in, time_out, hours_rendered
                              |               - tasks_accomplished, status ('verified' | 'pending')
                              |               - verified_by (Mentor User ID), verified_at
                              |
                              +--- 1:* --- [ojt_performance_records] (Midterm / Final Evaluations)

      |
      +--- 1:1 --- [accident_reports] (Medical care, injuries, immediate actions, witnesses)

   ====================================================================================================
   5. TECHNICAL IMPLEMENTATION STACK & REALTIME EVENT BUS
   ====================================================================================================

   1. FRONTEND ARCHITECTURE:
      - Framework: React 19 + Vite 6 Single Page Application (SPA).
      - Routing: React Router DOM v7 with animated transitions (`framer-motion`).
      - UI Design & Styling: Custom Vanilla Tailwind CSS tokens, dynamic Light/Dark mode (`ThemeProvider`),
      responsive dashboard layout with slide-out drawer on mobile and persistent sidebar on desktop.
      - Component System: Modular directory structure:
      * `/src/pages/admin/` (System audit, institutions, orgs, skill intelligence, users)
      * `/src/pages/institution/` (Directors, student intake, coordinators, monitoring, clearances)
      * `/src/pages/organization/` (Job management, talent applicants, DTR verification, mentors)
      * `/src/pages/student/` (Job discovery, DTR tracking, skills, complaints, career portfolio)
      * `/src/pages/public/` (Landing, role-specific registration gates, login, policy disclosures)

   2. BACKEND ARCHITECTURE:
      - Runtime: Node.js (ESM modules) with Express.js REST API.
      - Database: MySQL 8 (via `mysql2/promise` connection pool with automatic schema alignment migrations).
      - Authentication: Stateless JSON Web Tokens (JWT) signed with 64-character secret keys.
      - Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Two-tier middleware (`verifyToken` + `requireRole([allowed_roles])`).

   3. REALTIME WEBSOCKET EVENT BUS:
      - Engine: Socket.IO v4.
      - Events Emitted across Entities:
      * `job_updated`: Instant alert when opportunity is approved or updated.
      * `application_updated`: Live status updates on job applications.
      * `ojt_updated`: Instant sync when attendance hours are certified by mentors.
      * `notification_received`: Push notification bell update across all connected dashboards.
      * `complaint_updated`: Urgent dispatch of incident reports to coordinators.

   ====================================================================================================
   6. SUMMARY OF SYSTEM SAFEGUARDS & AUDIT MECHANISMS
   ====================================================================================================

   1. TAMPER-PROOF ATTENDANCE:
      Students cannot manually overwrite or forge their completed hours. Only the registered Workplace
      Mentor assigned to the student's company branch has the authorization to certify DTR logs.

   2. TRIPARTITE JOB APPROVAL CHECK:
      No organization can solicit students without academic sanction. The database strictly enforces
      that an institution approval record must exist before an opportunity is rendered searchable.

   3. RESUME AND BADGE AUTHENTICITY:
      Recruiters no longer need to conduct lengthy background checks for entry-level applicants. Any item
      marked `is_verified = 1` on an InternConPH portfolio is cryptographically tied to the verified
      organization ID that registered the student's internship or project.

   4. AUTOMATIC AUDIT LOGGING:
      All high-risk operations (approvals, rejections, password resets, access-code generations, and
      disciplinary complaints) are committed to the immutable `audit_logs` table capturing user ID,
      action timestamp, target entity, and client IP address.
