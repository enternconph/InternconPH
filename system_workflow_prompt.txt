2. PROOF OF LEGITIMACY (VERIFICATION DOCUMENTS)
===========================================================
To prove an institution, university, or business is legitimate and not a
fraud, the system must check for specific legal registrations, operating
licenses, and tax documents. Exact proof required per category in the
Philippines:

A. Proof Needed for Businesses
   - SEC Certificate of Registration: for corporations and partnerships,
     to prove legal personality.
   - DTI Certificate of Business Name Registration: for sole proprietorships.
   - Mayor's / Business Permit: issued by the LGU, showing permission to
     operate physically.
   - BIR Certificate of Registration (Form 2303): proves the business is
     registered for taxes and can issue official receipts.

B. Proof Needed for Colleges & Universities
   - CHED Government Recognition (GR): official certificate issued by CHED
     authorizing specific degree programs.
   - CHED Autonomous or Deregulated Status: highest institutional awards
     given to top-tier, highly trusted universities.
   - ALCUCOA, AACCUP, or PAASCU Certificates: third-party accreditation
     certificates showing high educational quality.
   - Listing in the CHED Official Registry: appearance on the active,
     public database of recognized higher education institutions.

C. Proof Needed for Schools & Tech-Voc Centers
   - DepEd Permit to Operate: required for elementary and high schools to
     legally teach students.
   - TESDA Certificate of TVET Program Registration (CTPR): mandatory for
     any center offering technical or vocational courses (e.g. caregiving,
     welding, culinary arts).

3. SYSTEM FLOW (STEP BY STEP)
===========================================================

--- STEP 1: Admin Verifies Institution / Hiring Organization Accounts ---
1. The admin verifies the accounts created by institutions and hiring
   organizations in the system.
2. Upon registering an account, it appears on the admin's dashboard along
   with the documents required for verification.
3. The admin can view the documents sent by the account creator.
4. The admin approves or rejects the applicant.
   - If rejected: the account is deleted and a notification is sent.
   - If approved: the applicant can now use their dashboard.

--- STEP 2: Institution Director Creates Institution Staff Accounts ---
1. The director verifies institution staff accounts (while for the hiring
   organization, HR verifies the Workplace Mentor).
2. When creating the account, the director inputs which program/department
   belongs to the institution.
3. To add staff, the director generates a passcode for the staff member to
   use to create an account.
   Passcode setup includes:
   - Name of the institution
   - Staff ID
   - Staff position
   - Which program the staff belongs to
   - What the staff can access
4. After generating the passcode, the director can send it to the staff
   member's Gmail so the staff can create their account.
5. When the staff registers, if what they fill up in the registration form
   matches what the director set up, the passcode grants them access to
   whatever was assigned to them.

Example of staff access/features:
   - OJT Supervisor/Adviser: sees ongoing OJT students, their performance,
     and complaints from the organization about the student.
   - Guidance Counselor: sees and handles Formal Grievances filed by
     students.
   - Registrar: provides soft copies of documents needed by students.
   - Dean: sees which staff are under their program and being handled.
   - Staff and the Dean can access and handle hiring organizations that
     sent OJT offers and job offers.

--- STEP 3: Institution Staff Verifies Student Accounts ---
1. Institution staff verifies students who create an account in the
   system.
2. For students who register as OJT, their status is verified by the OJT
   Supervisor.
3. Once they finish their OJT, they become "OJT Completer" and their
   status is updated.
4. When they graduate, their status is updated again along with what they
   can access.

How student verification works (similar to staff verification):
1. During registration, the form should ask:
   - Which program/department the student belongs to
   - Student type: Regular, Returnee, or Transferee
   - Whether the student is just starting OJT or has already finished OJT
     (so the system knows their status and can assign them to their OJT
     Supervisor based on their department/program)
2. Staff generates an access code for the student using the student's ID
   and program/department.
3. Even if the access code is correct, the account is NOT automatically
   approved — staff still needs to check the student's status.
4. Staff can view the status the student entered:
   - If status is Returnee or Transferee: it gets assigned to the
     Registrar.
   - If status is correct and the student has completed OJT: it gets
     approved and the student's dashboard is shown, but without the
     employer performance evaluation, since there's no OJT record yet.
   - For ongoing OJT students: the status should appear on the OJT
     Supervisor's dashboard as set during their registration.
5. If an account being created doesn't match what was set up by the
   Registrar and OJT Supervisor, the system rejects it and sends a
   notification to the account creator saying the passcode is incorrect
   and that no account was created for that institution.

--- STEP 4: Hiring Organization Gets Approved / Workplace Mentor Setup ---
1. HR can send an OJT posting in the system and select which institutions
   to send the offer to.
2. Depending on whether the institution accepts it: once accepted, it is
   automatically sent to students whose course matches the OJT being
   offered.
3. Before creating an OJT offer, HR must first create a Workplace Mentor
   account.
   - Same as before: when registering, an access code is required which
     must match the one generated for the Workplace Mentor account of the
     organization.
   - The content to be filled up during registration differs depending on
     which type of user is registering.
4. Once registered, the Workplace Mentor is added to the OJT offer to
   determine who will handle the students.

--- STEP 5: Job Posting Flow ---
1. Only HR handles creating job posts for job offers (not OJT).
2. Once the selected institutions accept the offer, it is sent to the
   students.
3. Students who have not graduated:
   - Can only view/browse the job post on their dashboard.
   - Cannot apply.
4. Only graduated students can apply.
5. Among applicants, HR can choose which students applied for OJT or a job.
6. HR can view the students' portfolios and resumes to see their
   achievements and skills, to decide who is applicable or the best
   choice among applicants.
7. HR can request an Interview.
8. If the interview is passed, HR can send important documents needed
   through the system.
9. HR can view OJT students and see their Evaluations, mark them as
   qualified candidates for job offers.
10. HR can send a job offer that only the selected student can receive.

--- STEP 6: Workplace Mentor Monitoring ---
1. Once HR selects students for OJT, the Workplace Mentor can monitor:
   - What time the student logged in.
   - How many hours were deducted from the required OJT hours.
2. The Workplace Mentor can send complaints if the OJT student:
   - Is not following rules.
   - Made an offense.
   - Caused a problem.
   The institution receives the complaint in order to send a warning to
   the student.
3. Once OJT hours are complete, the student submits Student Evaluations,
   rating the OJT's performance — this helps them when applying, since
   HR can view it.

--- STEP 7: Student Verification and Approval (Student Side) ---
1. If starting OJT:
   - The student can see who their assigned OJT Supervisor is, based on
     their department.
   - They can browse job posts but cannot apply if it's for full-time or
     part-time positions — only OJT postings can be applied to.
   - If the OJT offer doesn't match their course/skills, a notification
     pop-up will say they are not qualified for that offer.
2. For graduated students:
   - They can apply to job offers that were approved by the institution,
     offered by the hiring organization.
   - They can view their own portfolio and resume.
   - If they apply and get selected by HR, and HR requests an interview
     but the interview doesn't happen, they can file a Complaint Filing
     in the system.
   - This complaint is sent to the institution.
   - The institution can send a warning to the hiring organization — any
     type of complaint can be seen there.
