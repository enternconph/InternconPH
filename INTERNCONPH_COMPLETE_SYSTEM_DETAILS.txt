1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & PHILIPPINE LEGAL COMPLIANCE FRAMEWORK
========================================================================================================================

1.1 PROBLEM STATEMENT & STRATEGIC MISSION:
The internship and entry-level employment ecosystem in the Philippines has historically been fragmented, paper-reliant,
and exposed to systemic risks:
  - Students face exploitation (e.g., unpaid/unverified overtime, errand running unrelated to degree competencies).
  - Unaccredited diploma mills and fraudulent businesses operate without legal vetting.
  - SIPP requirements (tripartite agreements between schools, students, and employers) are difficult to track manually.
  - Student DTRs (Daily Time Records) are prone to ghost hours, forged mentor signatures, and misplaced physical logbooks.
  - Students facing workplace harassment or dangerous tasks have no safe channel to report issues without fearing retaliation.
  - Graduating students receive paper certificates that recruiters cannot easily authenticate.

InternConPH solves this by providing a unified digital infrastructure that enforces CHED, DOLE, and Data Privacy standards,
automates tripartite oversight, authenticates attendance via dynamic hardware-bound TOTP QR codes, and converts verified
internship achievements into permanent, cryptographically verifiable career portfolios.

1.2 CHED CMO NO. 104, SERIES OF 2017 (SIPP) MANDATES:
The Student Internship Program in the Philippines (SIPP) requires Higher Education Institutions (HEIs) and Host Training
Establishments (HTEs) to enforce strict compliance:
  - Tripartite Consensus: An internship cannot begin without approval from the HEI, HTE, and student.
  - Memorandums of Agreement (MOA): Documented institutional tie-ups between school and company.
  - Legitimate Host Establishments: Organizations must possess SEC/DTI registration, Mayor's Permit, and BIR Tax Registration.
  - Legitimate HEIs: Higher education institutions must possess CHED Government Recognition (GR), TESDA Certificate of
    Technical Program Registration (CTPR), or PACUCOA/AACCUP/PAASCU Level II/III accreditation.
  - Mandatory Hours Tracking: Strict recording of required curriculum hours (e.g., BSIT 486 hrs, BSBA 600 hrs, BSHM 500 hrs).
  - Grievance Committees: Established procedures for investigating internship disputes and safety hazards.

InternConPH's database and API routes enforce these rules at code level. No student can view or apply for an OJT opening
unless the institution has verified the student, approved the employer, and approved the specific job posting.

1.3 DOLE LABOR STANDARDS & ANTI-STUDENT EXPLOITATION SAFEGUARDS:
To uphold Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) standards:
  - Clear Distinction of Opportunities: Opportunities are segregated into 'ojt' (internship), 'career_job' (full-time/part-time
    employment for graduates), and 'on_call' (short-term deliverable projects with stipends).
  - Maximum Working Shift Enforcement: The attendance logging engine prohibits shifts exceeding 24 consecutive hours.
  - Mandatory Workplace Mentors: Companies must assign a dedicated supervisor to oversee tasks and certify DTR logs.
  - Automated Accident Reporting: Any on-site injury prompts an atomic incident report linking to medical facility details,
    witnesses, and preventive measures.

1.4 RA 10173 DATA PRIVACY COMPLIANCE & WHISTLEBLOWER SHIELDING:
In compliance with Republic Act No. 10173 (Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012):
  - Anonymized Student Grievances: When a student reports workplace sexual harassment, safety hazards, or labor violations,
    the report routes exclusively to their university Guidance Counselor and OJT Supervisor.
  - Identity Masking: When the university forwards the complaint to the employer for formal response, the student's personal
    identity is masked (`include_student_details = 0`). The employer sees: "Student Intern (Identity Protected by Institution)".
  - Scoped Access Control: Registrars cannot access grievance files; counselors cannot alter academic grades; mentors cannot
    view students' applications to competing organizations.

1.5 PHILIPPINE GEOGRAPHIC PRECISION (PSGC & PHIL-ADDRESS ENGINE):
Address inputs across all registrations, job postings, and student profiles are mapped to the Philippine Standard
Geographic Code (PSGC):
  - Standardized Regions (NCR, Region I to XIII, BARMM, CAR, CARAGA).
  - Provinces, Cities, Municipalities, and Barangays parsed through `psgc`, `phil-address`, `zipcodes-ph`, and `use-postal-ph`.
  - Phone validation strictly enforces Philippine mobile numbers (+63 9XX-XXX-XXXX format).

2.1 MULTI-TIER DISTRIBUTED TOPOLOGY:

  [ USER CLIENTS ]
        │
        ├── Desktop / Tablet Web Browser ──> [ React 19 + Vite 6 Single Page Application (SPA) ]
        │                                        Hosted on Vercel Global Edge CDN
        │
        └── Android / iOS Mobile Device  ──> [ React Native 0.76.2 Mobile Application ]
                                                 Dedicated for Deployed OJT Students (DTR, Dynamic TOTP QR, Journal)
                                                 │
                                                 ▼ (HTTP REST & Bearer Tokens)
  [ REVERSE PROXY / EDGE ]
        │
        ▼ (HTTPS / WSS Encrypted)
  [ BACKEND APPLICATION CLUSTER ]
        ├── Express.js 4.19.2 REST API Server (Node.js 20+ LTS)
        ├── Socket.IO v4 Realtime WebSocket Server (Bidirectional Event Bus)
        ├── Dynamic TOTP Cryptographic Engine (HMAC-SHA256, 30s Window, Constant-Time Verify)
        ├── AI Skills & Career Recommendation Engine (Domain Knowledge Graph + Google Gemini API)
        ├── Background Cron / Reminder Daemons (5-min Interview Alerts, Stale Session Pruning)
        ├── Google Meet REST API v2 Integration (OAuth2 Service Account Domain-Wide Delegation)
        └── Automated Database Self-Aligning Migration Engine (`migrate.js`)
              │
              ├── [ Managed MySQL 8.0 Relational Database Cluster ] (SSL/TLS, Connection Pool, Keep-Alive)
              ├── [ Cloudinary Media CDN ] (Document Archives, Avatars, PDF Proofs)
              └── [ Database Blob Fallback Storage (`stored_uploads`) ] (Container Disk Cache Re-Hydration)

2.2 HOSTING INFRASTRUCTURE:
  - Frontend Web Hosting: Vercel Global Edge Network (`https://internconph.vercel.app`).
    - Configured with `vercel.json` rewrite rules (`/(.*)` -> `/index.html`) to prevent 404s on deep client routes.
    - Automated zero-downtime deployments triggered by GitHub commits.
  - Backend API Hosting: Render Cloud Container Web Service (`https://internconph.onrender.com`).
    - Node.js 20 LTS managed environment with automatic process recovery.
    - Startup routine executes `runMigrations()`, `pruneStaleSessions()`, `checkGoogleMeetConfigOnStartup()`, and
      `startInterviewReminderService()`.
  - Database Hosting: Remote Managed MySQL 8.x Cluster (Aiven / Render Managed MySQL / TiDB / PlanetScale).
    - SSL/TLS mandatory encryption (`rejectUnauthorized: false`).
    - utf8mb4 encoding and Asia/Manila (+08:00) standard timezone.
  - Local Development Suite: Laragon WAMP Stack running on Windows.
    - Local MySQL 8.0 on `127.0.0.1:3306` (database: `interncon_ph`).
    - Dual runtime orchestrator via `npm run dev:all` (Vite port 5173, Express port 3000).

2.3 NETWORK SECURITY, SSL/TLS & CORS GOVERNANCE:
  - Cross-Origin Resource Sharing (CORS): Managed via dynamic origin callback in `server/src/config/cors.js`.
    - Whitelists localhost development origins (`localhost:5173`, `localhost:3000`, `127.0.0.1`).
    - Whitelists configured `FRONTEND_URL` and `ALLOWED_ORIGINS`.
    - Uses regex patterns to safely permit all `*.vercel.app` preview branch deployments and `*.onrender.com` services.
  - Session Security: JWT authentication tokens signed with HMAC-SHA256, with sessions recorded in `user_sessions`.
  - Rate Limiting & Protection: Password hashing via `bcryptjs` (salt rounds: 10), SQL parameterized queries on all endpoints.

2.4 DUAL-STRATEGY FILE STORAGE ARCHITECTURE:
  Because cloud container instances on free/ephemeral tiers clear disk storage upon container spin-down/redeployment,
  InternConPH implements a resilient dual-strategy storage layer:
  1. Primary: Files (resumes, SEC/DTI registrations, BIR permits, MOAs) uploaded via `multer` to `/uploads`.
  2. Persistent Backup: Every file upload is simultaneously saved to the `stored_uploads` MySQL table as `LONGBLOB` with
     its MIME type, original filename, and file path.
  3. Dynamic Re-Hydration: If an uploaded document is requested (`GET /uploads/:folder/:file`) and missing from local disk,
     the backend retrieves the `file_data` from MySQL, re-creates the file on disk for fast future static serving, and
     streams it to the client with appropriate `Content-Type` and `Cache-Control: 7d`.

========================================================================================================================
5. SYSTEM USERS, ROLES HIERARCHY & GRANULAR RBAC PERMISSIONS
========================================================================================================================

InternConPH implements a strict 6-tier Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) hierarchy governed by the `roles` table and
enforced at both backend middleware (`authenticateToken`, `requireRole`, `requireHROrAdmin`) and frontend client routes
(`ProtectedRoute`).

5.1 SYSTEM ADMINISTRATOR (`system_admin`):
  - Highest platform authority; independent of universities and corporations.
  - Vets and approves/rejects Institution and Organization registrations based on uploaded legal dossiers.
  - Reviews nationwide analytics, cross-discipline skill demand/supply dynamics, and platform-wide audit trails.
  - Adjudicates severe disputes escalated by HEIs against errant organizations.
  - Has administrative override to suspend or deactivate fraudulent accounts.

5.2 INSTITUTION DIRECTOR (`institution`):
  - Represents the College, University, or Technical-Vocational Institute (e.g., University President, Dean, or SIPP Director).
  - Configures institution profile, official campus address, and academic programs/curricula.
  - Governs the tripartite clearance gate: reviews and approves/rejects job postings targeted at their students.
  - Issues cryptographically hashed Access Passcodes to onboard Institution Staff with tailored permission sets.

5.3 INSTITUTION STAFF (`institution_staff`):
  - Sub-accounts operating under the HEI, categorized by official academic position:
    a) OJT Coordinator / Supervisor:
       - Monitors student progress during active deployment.
       - Reviews verified DTR attendance logs, hours remaining, and performance evaluations.
       - Issues academic warnings or schedules conferences upon receiving incident complaints from employers.
    b) College Registrar:
       - Verifies student identities, student numbers, and curriculum enrollment.
       - Clears Returnees and Transferees requiring special prerequisite audits.
       - Manages student clearance checklists and official academic certifications.
    c) Guidance Counselor:
       - Receives confidential student grievances and safety hazard reports.
       - Conducts mediation with employer HR while shielding student identity to prevent retaliation.
    d) Academic Dean:
       - High-level departmental oversight across curricula, faculty coordinators, and student batches.
       - Endorses college-wide internship agreements and industry tie-ups.

5.4 HIRING ORGANIZATION HR ADMINISTRATOR (`hiring_organization` / `hr_staff`):
  - Represents the corporate entity / Host Training Establishment (HTE).
  - Posts opportunities across three tracks: OJT (internships), Career Jobs (graduates), and On-Call Projects (gig stipends).
  - Designates which partner universities receive each opportunity posting.
  - Evaluates applicant pools, reviews verified portfolios, schedules interviews, and issues formal OJT/job offers.
  - Uses the on-site camera scanner (`HrAttendanceScannerModal`) to scan student dynamic TOTP QR codes.
  - Generates mentor access codes to onboard department heads and leads.

5.5 WORKPLACE MENTOR / SUPERVISOR (`workplace_mentor` / `mentor`):
  - Department lead, project manager, or senior engineer within the hiring company.
  - Directly supervises deployed interns on a day-to-day basis.
  - Certifies or rejects Daily Time Records (DTR), clock-in/out stamps, and rendered hours.
  - Submits Midterm and Final OJT Performance Evaluations with rubric ratings.
  - Submits workplace incident/misconduct reports or accident records directly to the HEI coordinator.
  - Prohibited from creating jobs, issuing legal employment offers, or editing corporate registration documents.

5.6 STUDENT CANDIDATE (`student`):
  - Candidates classified by enrollment status:
    - Regular Student: Follows standard curriculum timeline.
    - Returnee Student: Resuming studies after leave; requires registrar scholastic clearance.
    - Transferee Student: Transferred from another HEI; requires credit transfer validation.
  - Progresses across dynamic lifecycle states:
    1. Starting OJT: Registered and cleared; searching for approved internship opportunities matching degree program.
    2. Ongoing OJT Intern: Actively deployed at a verified HTE; logs daily DTR, presents dynamic TOTP QR, files journals.
    3. OJT Completer: Required hours fulfilled (e.g., 486/486 hrs) and verified; unlocked certificate and recommendation badges.
    4. Graduated Alumni / Career Candidate: Degree completed; unlocked to apply for full-time and part-time Career Jobs.

6. STEP-BY-STEP OPERATIONAL WORKFLOWS & STATE MACHINES
========================================================================================================================

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WORKFLOW 1: REGISTRATION, PROOF OF LEGITIMACY & ADMIN VETTING GATE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1.1: An organization or university representative visits the registration portal.
          - For Companies: Selects legal structure (Corporation, Sole Proprietorship, Partnership).
            Must provide SEC / DTI Registration Number, Mayor's Business Permit Number, BIR TIN (Form 2303).
            Must upload legal PDF documents via `POST /api/auth/register/organization`.
          - For HEIs: Selects classification (State University, Private HEI, Tech-Voc).
            Must provide CHED Institutional Code, Campus Address, and upload CHED Recognition / Charter Certificate
            via `POST /api/auth/register/institution`.
Step 1.2: System creates a record in `users` with `is_active = 0` (or `status = 'pending'`), records the entity in
          `hiring_organizations` or `institutions`, and creates an entry in `entity_registrations`.
Step 1.3: The user is locked out of operational dashboards and sees a waiting screen:
          "Registration Submitted for Legal Verification by System Administrator."
Step 1.4: System Administrator navigates to `/dashboard/admin/organizations` or `/dashboard/admin/institutions`.
          Admin clicks "View Dossier", inspects uploaded government documents directly via `/api/admin/documents/:id/view`.
Step 1.5: Admin clicks "Verify & Approve" or "Reject with Cause":
          - If Approved: Backend updates `is_verified = 1`, `status = 'active'`, and dispatches a realtime WebSocket
            notification (`notification_received`) and audit log entry. The user's full dashboard unlocks immediately.
          - If Rejected: Backend records detailed rejection notes in `entity_registrations.rejection_reason`.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WORKFLOW 2: CRYPTOGRAPHIC ACCESS CODES & DELEGATED INVITE GOVERNANCE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To prevent unauthorized users from registering under official universities or companies, InternConPH uses cryptographic
access passcodes:
Step 2.1: An authorized executive (Institution Director or Org HR) clicks "Invite Staff" or "Invite Mentor".
Step 2.2: Executive enters target user's details:
          - Target Identifier (Employee ID, School ID, or Corporate Email).
          - Intended Position (e.g., 'ojt_coordinator', 'registrar', 'counselor', 'mentor').
          - Assigned Permissions (JSON object of granular permissions).
Step 2.3: Backend generates a cryptographically secure random string, hashes it with SHA-256, and stores it in
          `access_codes` with an expiration timestamp (`expires_at`, typically 7 to 30 days).
Step 2.4: The executive securely provides the plaintext passcode (e.g., `INST-A8F2-99C1`) to the invitee.
Step 2.5: The invitee visits `/register/staff`, `/register/mentor`, or `/register/student`, enters the passcode along
          with their identifier.
Step 2.6: Backend validates:
          - Code exists, `is_used = 0`, and `expires_at > NOW()`.
          - Target identifier matches the applicant's entered ID.
          Upon successful registration, `access_codes.is_used` is set to 1, and `used_by_user_id` is linked.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WORKFLOW 3: STUDENT INTAKE, CLASSIFICATION, & ACADEMIC REGISTRAR/COORDINATOR CLEARANCE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 3.1: Student registers at `/register/student` using their university's access code.
          Selects Academic Degree Program, Classification (Regular, Returnee, Transferee), and initial OJT status.
Step 3.2: Record created in `students` with `is_verified = 0`.
Step 3.3: Institution Staff dashboard (`/dashboard/institution/students`) displays the student intake queue:
          - Regular students appear in the OJT Coordinator's queue for hours confirmation.
          - Returnees and Transferees appear in the Registrar's queue for prerequisite clearance.
Step 3.4: Staff reviews transcripts, confirms required hours quota (e.g., BSIT = 486 hrs), and clicks "Verify Student".
Step 3.5: Student's profile updates to `is_verified = 1`. Student's dashboard unlocks to explore approved OJT openings.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WORKFLOW 4: TRIPARTITE OPPORTUNITY CLEARANCE & PROGRAM ELIGIBILITY MATCHING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 4.1: HR drafts an Opportunity in `/dashboard/organization/jobs`:
          - Selects Track: `ojt` (Student Interns), `career_job` (Graduated Alumni), or `on_call` (Short-term Gig).
          - Configures Work Setup (Onsite, Remote, Hybrid), slots, salary/allowance, required skills, and finish time.
          - Designates the Workplace Mentor responsible for overseeing the position.
Step 4.2: Tripartite HEI Selection:
          - HR selects partner universities invited to receive this opportunity.
          - System creates pending records in `institution_job_approvals` for each selected school.
Step 4.3: The posting is completely hidden from students until an Institution Director or Coordinator reviews it!
Step 4.4: Institution Coordinator visits `/dashboard/institution/ojt-offers`:
          - Inspects job description, assigned mentor, working conditions, and safety measures.
          - Clicks "Approve & Endorse for Curriculum".
Step 4.5: System queries `job_required_programs` and alerts eligible students via realtime WebSocket push that an
          approved, institution-cleared OJT opening is now live.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WORKFLOW 5: CANDIDATE MATCHING, APPLICATION PIPELINE, INTERVIEW PIPELINE (GOOGLE MEET) & FORMAL OFFERS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 5.1: Student discovers opportunities in `/dashboard/student/jobs`.
          - System calculates an algorithmic match score based on `student_skills` vs `job_required_skills`.
          - If an active OJT student attempts to apply for a `career_job`, the submission is blocked with an alert:
            "Career jobs are strictly reserved for Graduated Alumni."
Step 5.2: Student submits application (cover letter, verified portfolio items, active resume from `student_resumes`).
Step 5.3: Application record inserted in `job_applications` with status `'pending'`.
Step 5.4: HR reviews applicant dossiers in `/dashboard/organization/applicants`:
          - Updates status: `'pending'` -> `'reviewing'` -> `'shortlisted'`.
Step 5.5: HR clicks "Schedule Interview":
          - Selects date, time, mode (`'online'`, `'onsite'`, `'phone'`).
          - If mode is `'online'`, HR can click "Generate Google Meet Link".
          - Backend calls Google Meet REST API v2 via Domain-Wide Delegation to auto-create a space, saving the link
            in `interviews.meeting_link`. (If unconfigured, gracefully falls back to custom link entry).
          - Interview record created in `interviews`. Realtime alert delivered to student.
Step 5.6: Five minutes before the scheduled interview, the backend daemon (`interviewReminder.service.js`) dispatches
          an automated reminder push notification to both student and recruiter with direct meeting link.
Step 5.7: Formal Offer & Tripartite Deployment:
          - If selected, HR issues an offer via `POST /api/org/offers` (stored in `ojt_deployment_offers`).
          - Student accepts offer: System creates an official training program in `ojt_records`:
            `required_hours` set to curriculum quota, `rendered_hours = 0.00`, status `'ongoing'`.
          - Student transitions to "Ongoing OJT Intern".

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WORKFLOW 6: ACTIVE OJT DEPLOYMENT, DTR DAILY ATTENDANCE & MENTOR HOUR CERTIFICATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 6.1: Active interns log attendance through two synchronized methods:
          Method A: Dynamic Hardware-Bound TOTP QR Code (Recommended On-Site Method):
            - Student opens mobile app or web modal (`StudentAttendanceQrModal`).
            - Client generates a 20/30-second rotating QR code payload containing `{ sid, dt, ts, sig }`, signed with
              the student's unique secret key via HMAC-SHA256, bound to their locked hardware device token (`dt`).
            - Recruiter/Mentor uses camera scanner (`HrAttendanceScannerModal`) to scan the QR code.
            - Backend `verifyTotpPayload()` validates timestamp (rejects screenshots older than 30s) and hardware token.
            - Automatically clocks the intern in or out in `ojt_attendance_logs`.
          Method B: Self-Reported DTR Shift Submission:
            - Intern submits log entry with `log_date`, `time_in`, `time_out`, `tasks_accomplished`, and `hours_rendered`.
            - Status defaults to `'pending'`.
Step 6.2: Workplace Mentor reviews attendance logs in `/dashboard/organization/ojt`:
          - Mentor clicks "Verify & Credit":
            * `ojt_attendance_logs.status` becomes `'verified'`.
            * `ojt_records.rendered_hours` increments by the verified hours.
            * `students.completed_ojt_hours` synchronizes.
            * Student receives realtime alert of credited hours and remaining balance.
          - Mentor can also add manual hours adjustments via `POST /api/org/interns/:ojtId/hours` for overtime or
            special project credits with full audit logging.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WORKFLOW 7: PERFORMANCE EVALUATION & AUTOMATED VERIFIABLE CAREER PORTFOLIO CREDITING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 7.1: When `rendered_hours >= required_hours`, the student completes their hour quota.
Step 7.2: Workplace Mentor submits Midterm / Final evaluation in `ojt_performance_records`:
          - Evaluates standardized rubric criteria: Quality of Work, Dependability, Technical Skills, Ethics.
          - System calculates numeric rating (e.g., 4.90 / 5.00) with written commendations.
Step 7.3: Automated Certificate & Portfolio Generation (`checkAndGenerateCertificate()`):
          - Upon evaluation submission and hour completion, system automatically generates an official certificate in
            `ojt_certificates` with a unique serial code (`CERT-OJT-YYYY-XXXX-RAND`).
          - Automatically binds the credential to the student's Career Portfolio (`portfolio_items`) with `is_verified = 1`,
            linked to the authentic hiring organization ID.
          - When the student graduates, future recruiters can inspect these verified badges with zero fraud risk.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WORKFLOW 8: BIDIRECTIONAL INCIDENT REPORTING, ACCIDENT LOGGING & WHISTLEBLOWER GRIEVANCE SHIELDING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Scenario A: Organization Filing Misconduct / Safety Report Against Intern:
  - If intern commits unexcused absences, safety violations, or misconduct, HR/Mentor files report via `POST /api/org/complaints`.
  - If a physical injury occurred on-site, an `accident_reports` record is atomically created (injury description, medical
    attention given, facility, witnesses, preventive measures).
  - Complaint is transmitted EXCLUSIVELY to the university OJT Coordinator.
  - Coordinator investigates, schedules a conference, and records disciplinary notes or formal warnings.

Scenario B: Student Filing Grievance Against Employer (Whistleblower Protection):
  - If an intern encounters harassment, hazardous duties, or unpaid overtime, student files report via `POST /api/student/complaints`.
  - Complaint routes directly and exclusively to the University Guidance Counselor.
  - Privacy Shielding: When the counselor forwards the report to the employer for formal response, the university toggles
    `include_student_details = 0`. The company sees: "Student Intern (Identity Protected by Institution)".
  - If an employer exhibits repeat critical violations, the HEI escalates the dossier to the System Administrator to suspend
    or revoke the company's platform accreditation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WORKFLOW 9: OFFICIAL OJT CERTIFICATE GENERATION & PUBLIC CRYPTOGRAPHIC VERIFICATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 9.1: An official certificate is generated upon fulfillment of hours and evaluation sign-off.
Step 9.2: Certificate serial code format: `CERT-OJT-[YEAR]-[OJT_ID]-[4_CHAR_RANDOM_HASH]` (e.g. `CERT-OJT-2026-0042-8K9P`).
Step 9.3: Anyone (student, prospective employer, accreditation auditor) can verify the certificate via two public endpoints:
          - `GET /api/certificates/verify/:code` -> Returns JSON verification payload confirming student name, school,
            company, rendered hours, rating, and issue timestamp.
          - `GET /api/certificates/render/:code` -> Renders an official, printable SVG/HTML security certificate with
            embossed seals, QR code, and Philippine SIPP compliance marks.

7.3 DYNAMIC SELF-ALIGNING MIGRATION ENGINE (`server/src/config/migrate.js`):
  InternConPH includes an automatic schema migration engine executed on server startup (`runMigrations()`).
  Rather than requiring manual database intervention, it:
  - Creates all 61 tables if they do not already exist (`CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS`).
  - Introspects existing table columns via `DESCRIBE [table_name]` and dynamically appends missing columns via `ALTER TABLE`.
  - Ensures critical foreign keys and performance indexes (`idx_ac_lookup`, `idx_auditlog_user`, `idx_job_org`, etc.) exist.
  - Automatically migrates and populates the master programs catalog (`master_programs`), role definitions (`roles`),
    and standardized complaint categories (`complaint_categories`).

  - Encodes a 20-second rotating cryptographic payload: `{ sid, dt, ts, sig }`.
  - Features an animated visual progress ring counting down 20 seconds, after which the QR code automatically re-signs and refreshes.
  - Includes a drifting holographic watermark ("INTERNCONPH AUTHENTIC") across the QR canvas to make phone screen video/photo

    persisted under `@internconph_server_url`.

11. ADVANCED CORE SUBSYSTEMS & TECHNICAL HIGHLIGHTS
========================================================================================================================

11.1 TIME-BOUND DYNAMIC QR ATTENDANCE & HARDWARE DEVICE TOKEN BINDING:
  - Problem Solved: Buddy punching, screenshot sharing, and attendance forgery.
  - Cryptographic Implementation (`server/src/utils/totp.helper.js`):
    * Device Registration: When an intern first launches the QR modal, a unique 48-character hardware device token is
      generated and locked to their device in `student_attendance_devices`.
    * HMAC-SHA256 Signing: Client signs the payload `sid:dt:timestamp` using the student's unique 64-character secret.
    * Time Window Verification: Server parses `{ sid, dt, ts, sig }`. It calculates `abs(currentServerTime - ts)`.
      If the time difference exceeds 30 seconds, the scan is rejected:
      "QR code expired. Screenshots are strictly prohibited. Please scan a live dynamic QR code."
    * Constant-Time Signature Equality: Uses `crypto.timingSafeEqual()` to prevent timing attacks.
    * Hardware Mismatch Protection: If a student screenshots their QR and sends it to a coworker on another phone, the
      device token mismatch causes an immediate rejection.

11.2 AI CAREER & SKILLS RECOMMENDATION ENGINE:
  - Implementation (`server/src/services/aiRecommendation.service.js`):
    * Multi-Discipline Domain Knowledge Graph: Encodes baseline skills, certifications, and industry standards across
      15+ academic disciplines (BSIT, BSCS, BSCpE, BSA, BSBA, BSCE, BSEE, BSHM, BSTM, BSN, BSPSY, BSEd, etc.).
    * Companion Synergy Matrix: Determines skill-to-skill synergy scores (e.g., React.js + Node.js = 99% Full-Stack synergy;
      AutoCAD + Revit = 98% BIM synergy; Financial Accounting + QuickBooks = 99% Cloud Accounting synergy).
    * Philippine Labor Market Intelligence: Embeds entry-level Philippine salary ranges (PHP) and hiring demand rankings.
    * Dual-Engine Architecture: Calls Google Gemini API (`gemini-1.5-flash`) for real-time generative career path synthesis,
      with an automatic zero-latency fallback to the internal semantic knowledge graph if API keys are unconfigured.

11.3 OFFICIAL OJT COMPLETION CERTIFICATE GENERATION & VERIFICATION ENGINE:
  - Implementation (`server/src/services/certificate.service.js` & `server/src/routes/certificate.routes.js`):
    * Automatic Trigger: Fired immediately when an intern's rendered hours meet or exceed the curriculum quota and the
      workplace mentor submits the final rubric evaluation.
    * Unique Serial Generation: Produces formatted serial `CERT-OJT-[YEAR]-[OJT_ID]-[RANDOM_HASH]`.
    * Automatic Portfolio Crediting: Automatically injects an authenticated credential into the student's lifelong
      Career Portfolio (`portfolio_items`) with `is_verified = 1`, bound to the authentic hiring organization ID.
    * Public Verification Endpoints:
      - `GET /api/certificates/verify/:code` -> Returns verified JSON payload.
      - `GET /api/certificates/render/:code` -> Renders official certificate view with Philippine SIPP compliance seals.

11.4 GOOGLE MEET REST API V2 INTEGRATION WITH DOMAIN-WIDE DELEGATION:
  - Implementation (`server/src/services/googleMeet.service.js`):
    * Direct Server-Side Integration: Communicates directly with `https://meet.googleapis.com/v2/spaces`.
    * Authentication Schemes: Supports Google Workspace Service Account with Domain-Wide Delegation (native RSA-SHA256
      JWT assertion with user impersonation) or OAuth2 Refresh Token exchange.
    * Fault Tolerance & Graceful Degradation: If Google credentials are not configured, the interview scheduler seamlessly
      and gracefully falls back to custom meeting links (Zoom, Teams, manual Meet links) without throwing errors.

11.5 REAL-TIME WEBSOCKET EVENT BUS (SOCKET.IO V4 MATRIX):
  - Events emitted across entities via `emitUpdate(entity, id, action, data)`:
    * `job_updated`: Pushed when an opportunity is created, edited, or endorsed by an institution.
    * `application_updated`: Pushed when a candidate applies, is shortlisted, or receives an offer.
    * `interview_updated`: Pushed when an interview schedule is created or modified.
    * `offer_updated`: Pushed when an OJT deployment or career employment offer is dispatched or answered.
    * `ojt_updated`: Pushed when an intern clocks in/out or a mentor certifies attendance hours.
    * `complaint_updated`: Urgent dispatch of incident reports or safety notices to coordinators.
    * `notification_received`: Triggers the red notification badge on the user's header in real time.

11.6 EPHEMERAL CONTAINER FILE RECOVERY ARCHITECTURE (`stored_uploads`):
  - Solves the problem of ephemeral disk resets on cloud container platforms (e.g. Render restarts).
  - Every file uploaded to `/uploads` is simultaneously saved as a `LONGBLOB` in `stored_uploads`.
  - When a file request arrives and is missing from disk, Express intercepts the 404, retrieves the binary data from MySQL,
    writes the file back to the disk cache, and streams it with full headers (`Cache-Control: public, max-age=604800`).

11.7 AUTOMATED 5-MINUTE INTERVIEW REMINDER DAEMON:
  - Implementation (`server/src/services/interviewReminder.service.js`):
    * Background interval checks the `interviews` table for scheduled sessions starting within the next 5 minutes.
    * Dispatches instant push notifications to both the student candidate and recruiter user accounts with direct meeting link.
    * Sets `reminder_sent_5m = 1` atomically to guarantee zero duplicate alerts across concurrent clusters.
